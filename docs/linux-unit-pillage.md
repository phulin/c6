# Ordinary improvement Pillage

`c6_data::loaded_actions::session::Session::pillage` supports a bounded Linux XP2 improvement Pillage request on the current loaded state and persistent runtime owners. It does not implement complete Civ VI state, arbitrary actions or turns. District and building pillaging remain unsupported.

Construct `loaded_actions::pillage::Definitions`, `plunder::Definitions` and `notification::Inputs` from the admitted rules. Supply the current callback registrations through `stock::Inputs`, then assemble `current::Inputs` and `current::Dispatcher` using the same movement, placement, reporting, Script, modifier and city-query owners as the Session. The city-query territory must use its retained shared owner: reporting can consume a temporary PlotYields identity through a short mutable loan. A copied counter or replacement territory runtime is not equivalent. The Session reborrows its own visibility history for Gossip.

Supply the issuer and Unit identity as the external player decision. The request passes through the existing operation queue, current handler legality, synchronous callbacks, deactivation, diplomacy and notification aftermath to `HeadlessMessagesProcessed`. It spends movement and awards plunder without ending the turn or selecting native AI policy. Handler-level `pillage::can_start` is narrower than the complete manager and Script legality checks executed by the Session request.

The checked native case is a damaged Warrior pillaging an enemy human player's city-owned Farm on retained floodplains. Native and Rust agree on healing from 35 damage to zero; Unit and Formation movement from two moves to zero; Farm pillage state; Player statistics; GlobalAI Mayhem; modifier and temporary PlotYields identities; the completed empty queue, awake activity and zero timer; and the retained known RNG streams. Genuine pending enemy CityProduction and active-player CommandUnits decisions remain present. This is representative action coverage against the pinned [Linux target](targets.md), not general improvement, world or turn equivalence.

The informational TilePillaged notice uses the explicit stock headless scope with no added consumers. Its source-qualified nonblocking, nonautomatic publication becomes a semantic notice; native display text, UI occurrence pools and queued frontend playback are outside that scope. Required gameplay effects of reporting still run, including temporary PlotYields creation/removal. Empty Lua hooks retain their logical registry effects rather than replaying native callback answers.

Nonhealing plunder scaling and yield awards, district/building pillaging, nonempty Gossip or callback VM work, positive unsupported modifier removal, resource/Economic/FreeCities/barbarian branches, and additional healing or loyalty continuations remain explicit unsupported paths. A reached failure retains its applied prefix and freezes the Session. Inspect or extract that state before continuing; an incomplete transition is never reported as a completed Pillage action.
