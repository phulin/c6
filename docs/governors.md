# First governor trajectory

The bounded Session surface supports the first ordinary Pingala appointment, an explicit assignment to an owned revealed city, and establishment through ordinary player turns. The rules must provide Pingala's ordinary Librarian base ability with unconditional city yield modifiers. Other governors, subsequent appointments, promotions, reassignment/removal, foreign-city assignment and neutralization remain unsupported. This is not complete Civ VI state, actions or turns.

Before a major title-grant callback, bind the initialized player's actual unsaved maximum with `LoadedState::bind_governor_current(owner, governor_lifecycle::Current { maximum_points })`. Saved governor points do not establish this value. Research acquisition uses the ordinary retained modifier owner, caps saved points at this initialized maximum and updates title-considered state. A title never selects a governor or a city.

Compile `loaded_actions::governor_actions::Definitions::from_rules` against the current state and rules. At an ordinary player-action boundary, `Session::pending_governor_decision` exposes the supported first appointment or currently legal owned-city assignments. `appoint_governor` and `assign_governor` execute the selected choices; `defer_governor_title` explicitly defers a title. Assignment requires the current loyalty query context and definitions used by the same initialized world. The stock scope omits presentation cache publication and requires no active governor requirement subscribers or custom consumers of summary data. Other live subscriber graphs need their current effects implementation.

Assignment immediately contributes the governor's identity pressure to loyalty and recalculates conversion trends. Establishment preserves the ordered city/site map and executes during the ordinary governor upkeep position after unit upkeep. On establishment, the existing persistent modifier owner attaches Librarian's rules-defined science and culture yield modifiers. Yield calculations read those canonical city fields on subsequent queries and turns; no independent governor yield model or policy engine is used.

Unsupported inputs or reached effects fail explicitly. A callback failure can retain a partially executed action or turn; the Session retains that failure and refuses further advancement. Inspect the retained state instead of interpreting a partial prefix as completed gameplay.

Dev and release comparisons against the pinned Linux XP2 stock fixture match at all 14 checkpoints across eight externally controlled rounds. Pingala establishes on round 5, retains five turns on site, and applies the rules-defined +15% science and culture modifiers. Assignment contributes identity pressure 8 immediately. The comparison checks saved gameplay prefixes, complete saved Governor and City records, live fields, persistent modifier identities and subscriptions, callback order, GameSync and observed auxiliary RNG states.

This establishes the bounded first-Pingala trajectory under that callback profile. Native planning suffix differences, raw requirement reference equality, an unclassified governor runtime word and a complete RNG inventory remain explicit coverage limits; arbitrary governor actions and complete state/turn equivalence are not established. Synthetic checks also cover title clamping, recycled identities, deferred assignment and site-map boundaries. See [targets.md](targets.md) for the Linux target identity.
